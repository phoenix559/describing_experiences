Giving Personal Information

Warm-Up: Birthday vs. Birthdate

Your birthday is the anniversary of your birth (e.g., May 20). Your birthdate (or date of birth) is the day and year when you were born (e.g., May 20, 1983).

Brainstorming Activity: Find the birthdate of one famous person and share it.

    Common Questions

    Listen to your teacher say these common questions. Practice saying them out loud.

    • Can I get some information/info?
    • What's your first name?
    • What's your last name?
    • Do you have a middle name?
    • What's your home number?
    • What's your cell number?
    • What's your work number?
    • What's your birthdate?
    • What's your email address?
    • Is this your current mailing address?

    Dialogue Practice

    Practice with a partner. Then change the personal information to your own information.

    Dialogue 1: Names

    👩🏽 Can I have your last name, please?
    It's Williams. W-I-L-L-I-A-M-S. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 And your first name?
    Corey. C-O-R-E-Y. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 Do you have a middle name?
    No, I don't. 👨🏻

    Dialogue 2: Phone Numbers & Birthdates

    👩🏽 Can I have your phone number, please?
    Sure. It's 555-222-7798. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 555-222-7798. Is that your home number?
    That's my cell. I don't have a home phone. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 Okay. Do you have a work number?
    Yes. It's 414-990-4878. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 And your birthdate is May 5, 1987, right?
    That's correct. 👨🏻

    Dialogue 3: Addresses

    👩🏽 Is this your current mailing address?
    No, we moved. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 What's your new address?
    My address is 45 Arrow Drive. Unit 88. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 Is that in Buffalo?
    Yes. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 What's your zip code?
    My zip code is 14215. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 Great. And is your email address still coreywilliams@mailme.com?
    That's right. 👨🏻

    Correct Info?

    Sometimes people already have your information. They may check to make sure it's still correct.

    👩🏽 And your birthdate is May 5, 1987, right?
    That's correct. 👨🏻

    You can answer in a few ways:

    • That's right.
    • Right.
    • That's correct.
    • You got it.
    • Yes.

    Spelling

    Are you giving personal information over the phone or in a loud place? It's useful to spell out difficult or important words. Use familiar nouns or the English Phonetic Spelling Alphabet.

    👩🏽 What's your last name?
    Chia. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 Can you spell that for me, please?
    Yep. It's C as in Charlie, H as in Hotel, I as in India, A as in Alfa. 👨🏻
    👩🏽 C-H-I-A?
    That's correct. 👨🏻
